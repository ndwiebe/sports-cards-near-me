## Notes on confidence and exclusions

**Directory sourcing:** Most rows came from two card-shop directory sites, thecardshopfinder.com and cardshows.io, which were spot-checked against independent Google/Yelp/Reddit results (Bleecker Trading, Dave & Adam's, E and J Boutique, World Champion Trading Cards, Squiggy's Dugout, Card Cave Central, and others all matched independently before the directories were trusted more broadly). Every row's own website (where present) was pulled directly from the directory page and is the primary source; the directory listing URL is the secondary source. Rows marked "low" either lack a confirmed official website, lack a confirmed street address, or come from a single Yelp/Mapquest/Facebook snippet rather than a dedicated business page.

**"Nxt Legaci" vs "Nxt Legaci Gaming"** — cardshows.io lists these as two separate, currently-open NYC locations at two different addresses (526 7th Ave vs 200 W 41st St). Kept as two rows; flag for Nathan in case one is stale/relocated data.

**"Collector Cave"** appears twice (Bronx and Yonkers) under the same website (collectorcave.shop) — likely a small local chain, kept as two separate storefronts.

**Forbidden Planet** and **The Compleat Strategist** are NYC comic-book/tabletop-wargaming institutions, not sports-card-first shops, but both are directory-tagged as carrying Pokemon/Magic/sports-card singles — included at low confidence, flag for Nathan to decide fit.

**Mint Models** (Albany) — the name suggests scale models, but cardshows.io categorizes it specifically as a "Sports Cards" store with a 4.9 rating; no independent second source found, so kept at low confidence only.

**732collectiblez** (Edison, NJ) — directory-listed with a real Google review count, but the shop's own domain did not resolve (DNS failure) at time of research and no street address is published; kept at low confidence, flag for Nathan to verify before adding.
