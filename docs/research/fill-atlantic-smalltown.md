## Coverage notes

**Nova Scotia (25 towns searched):** New finds in Dartmouth, Truro (x2), New Minas, Antigonish. **No shop found** (multiple query variants each): Bridgewater, Port Hawkesbury, Glace Bay, Sydney Mines, Windsor, Liverpool, Digby, Pictou, Stellarton, Springhill, Berwick, Middleton, Shelburne, Lunenburg, Chester, Elmsdale, Lower Sackville, Cole Harbour, Eastern Passage, Amherst (only a card/gift show Facebook page and generic sporting-goods store turned up, both excluded).

**New Brunswick (19 towns searched):** New finds in Riverview (x2), Miramichi (x3), Woodstock, Sackville (x2), Caraquet, plus Moncton (adjacent to Dieppe/Shediac searches). **No shop found:** Dieppe (existing D&M Sports Cards / Trading Card Warehouse entries only), Edmundston, Campbellton (an unnamed mall-booth "collectibles vendor" was seen but excluded per the no-name-no-include rule), Sussex, Grand Falls, Shediac, Quispamsis, Rothesay, Rogersville, Tracadie, Dalhousie, St. Stephen, Hampton, Bouctouche.

**PEI (9 searches, incl. Charlottetown re-sweep):** This was the biggest win — 4 new shops found (Summerside, Stratford, and a Charlottetown shop that directories had mis-filed under Montague/Georgetown). **No shop found:** Cornwall, Kensington, Alberton. The Charlottetown re-sweep turned up only already-known shops (Downtown Cards, Backmandy Collectibles, Gifted Rookies, EM Cards, Exor Games) plus Sherwood Sports Cards (new, listed above). PEI genuinely does appear thin outside Charlottetown/Summerside/Stratford — most small towns (Cornwall, Kensington, Alberton) had zero local retail presence, only Facebook Marketplace private sellers.

**Newfoundland & Labrador (14 towns searched):** New finds in Gander, Happy Valley-Goose Bay, Paradise. **No shop found:** Grand Falls-Windsor, Labrador City, Conception Bay South (same chain as Paradise — Noble Miniatures — not double-counted), Carbonear, Clarenville, Marystown, Stephenville, Torbay, Portugal Cove-St. Philip's, Bonavista, Placentia.

**Overall assessment: genuinely sparse, not just under-searched.** Every town got 2-4 distinct query variants (business-name searches, "sports card shop <town>", directory cross-checks) via Yahoo/Brave/DuckDuckGo web search and direct site fetches. Most small Atlantic towns (pop. under ~10k) simply have no dedicated card retail — only Facebook Marketplace private sellers turn up. The real supply is concentrated in the larger service towns (Truro, Miramichi, Riverview/Moncton, Summerside/Stratford/Charlottetown, Gander), which is exactly where this pass found new shops. PEI was the strongest confirmation of "under-searched" — 4 real shops surfaced against a starting roster of just 5, all in Charlottetown.
