## Notes on confidence and exclusions

**Grosnor Distribution Inc** and **Universal Distribution** are wholesale trading-card distributors (supply retailers, not the public) that describe a walk-in cash-and-carry showroom — included at low confidence because that's a different business model than a normal shop; flag for Nathan to decide if wholesale-with-public-access fits the directory.

**Hall Of Fame Grading** may be a grading-submission drop-off point rather than a retail storefront — no product list or website found, only a Yellow Pages category match.

**Break-Away Sports Cards Inc** (Rodney) and **Gimme Mo' / Bardown Hockey / Lasting Memories** are rural-route or residential-street addresses — plausibly legitimate home-based dealers, but storefront status is unconfirmed.
